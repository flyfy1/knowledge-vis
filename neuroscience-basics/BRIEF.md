# 神经科学基础 · build brief

Four-step bilingual introduction: resting potential, graded potential vs threshold, action-potential all-or-none behavior, and refractory-period firing ceiling.

- Core API: `restingPotential`, `gradedPotential`, `spikeState`, `maxRate`.
- Scope: simplified single neuron; no full Hodgkin–Huxley dynamics, synaptic networks or clinical claims.
- Validation: `node tests/core.cjs`, `node --check app.js`, browser sliders/language/quiz.
