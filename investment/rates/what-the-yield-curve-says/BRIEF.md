## Chapters (law · model)
1. 零息债券 — bond = bundle of zeros on the ACM zero curve of a chosen date; YTM vs z(T) (coupon effect).
2. 远期利率 — f = (z₂t₂ − z₁t₁)/(t₂ − t₁); lock-in units; arbitrage profit against a quoted forward.
3. 预期加期限溢价 — year-by-year expected path (drag on canvas or sliders) + linear TP; second panel: path implied by another TP from the same curve (exact inverse).
4. 远期不是预报 — one-year hold of an n-year zero; 'forwards realised' ⇒ return = 1y rate, 'unchanged' ⇒ return = forward; breakeven shift. Second lab: realised annual excess return of the ACM 10y zero over the 1y zero, Jan→Jan, 1962–2025.
5. 期限溢价 — ACM 10y decomposition + Kim–Wright, monthly 1990–2026 (data view, no toy model).
6. 久期与 DV01 — annual-compounding bond; exact vs duration vs duration+convexity.
7. 凸性与免疫 — 7-year liability, bullet vs barbell (weights match duration), parallel shift and linear twist; continuous compounding, flat 4.5 %.
8. 水平、斜率、曲率 — PCA of monthly changes of ACM 1–10y zeros (computed in tools/make-data.cjs, Jacobi); Nelson–Siegel least-squares fit (λ = 1.37 y, Diebold–Li) to the Treasury par curve on three dates.
9. 为什么会倒挂 — ACM split of 10y − 1y into expectations and TP, 2000–2026; daily inversion dates from T10Y3M / T10Y2Y (runs merged across < 30 positive trading days). Recession record: one paragraph + link to sibling ch4.
10. 信用利差 — annual-step defaultable zero (recovery of face); expected-loss spread vs risk-neutral spread (multiplier k); S&P 5y cumulative → annual PD; ICE OAS comparison.
11. 评级与垃圾债 — Vasicek one-factor Monte Carlo (seed 20261125, 2000 years, stepped on dt); S&P annual speculative-grade default rates 1981–2024 overlaid.
12. 前沿在哪 — tagged 已确立 / 已演示 / 某方声称.
13. 五个问题 — usable ending.
Diagrams (8, `tools/diagrams.cjs`): strips, forward routes, stack (model), seesaw (model), barbell (model), PCA shapes (data), credit tree, rating ladder (data).
