## Steps
**0 Stocks and flows.** Knows: carbon is in air, plants, sea. Question: how big is each reservoir and how long does carbon stay? Input: box choice; seek stock, in/out flux, turnover = stock/out. Controls: five buttons highlight a box; arrow thickness ∝ √flux. Holds: round numbers, simplified.
**1 Small imbalance.** Knows: ledger. Question: effect of a 3% mismatch? Input p (−5..5 %); seek net = 120·p% GtC/yr, ppm/yr = net/2.12 and share of 10 GtC. Controls: slider tilts the orange line; red is emissions. Holds: linear 50-year view, no feedback.
**2 Emissions accumulate.** Knows: ledger. Question: how fast does CO₂ climb? Inputs E 0–15, year; seek ppm, cumulative, airborne fraction. Controls: sliders. Holds: toy 5-box model (β=0.15, Revelle 10, 2.12 GtC/ppm).
**3 Where it goes.** Knows: climb. Question: where does the carbon end up? Inputs E, t; seek air+land+ocean = cumulative (conservation). Controls: sliders resize the stacked bar.
**4 After stopping.** Knows: where it goes. Question: does CO₂ drop at once? Input stop year; seek excess at stop and 100 yr later. Controls: slider moves the stop line. Holds: no climate feedback, constant 10 before stop.
**5 Scenarios.** Knows: all. Question: how do paths differ at year 150? Buttons: four paths; seek peak, year-150 ppm, cumulative. Links: photosynthesis#step2, energy-pyramid#step0, climate-energy#step2, climate-feedback#step0.

## First-appearance terms
GtC, reservoir/stock, flux, residence time (0); net flux, ppm conversion (1); airborne fraction, CO₂ fertilisation, ocean buffer (2); conservation of carbon (3); long tail (4).

## Simulation vs reality
Five boxes with first-order exchange; no temperature feedback, acidification, weathering or land-use; round-number stocks (fossil ≈ 4000 GtC is an uncertain resource estimate); parameters tuned, not fitted; trends only, not forecasts.
