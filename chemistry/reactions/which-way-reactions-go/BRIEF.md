## The one question
What decides whether a reaction goes by itself, how far it goes, and how fast?

## Chapters (content.zh.md is complete for all twelve)
1. Energy is not the judge. Entropy ledger ΔS_total = ΔS − ΔH/T (Clausius). Model `ledger`: ice, water → vapour, CaCO3.
2. Entropy counts arrangements. S = k ln W; Einstein solids Ω = C(q+N−1, q); model `split` (exact distribution) and
   `solidSim` (microcanonical random walk, symmetric moves so microstates are uniform).
3. Boltzmann factor derived by counting the bath's states (`bath`: exact P(n) ∝ Ω_bath(q−n) against r^n, r = q/(q+N));
   two-level populations (`boltzRatio`).
4. ΔG = ΔH − TΔS as −TΔS_total; ten real reactions on the ΔH–ΔS plane, the ΔG° = 0 line rotating with T.
5. Chemical potential, extent of reaction; G(ξ) of N2O4 ⇌ 2NO2 with its mixing-entropy minimum (`dissoc`).
6. ΔrG = ΔG° + RT ln Q, K = e^(−ΔG°/RT), Le Chatelier as a consequence, van 't Hoff (`lnK`; NH3 line against Shomate).
7. Rate is separate. Eyring equation; A ⇌ B with 400 molecules (`twoState`, `flipSim`); a catalyst leaves K alone.
8. Coupling: ATP (ΔG°′ + RT ln Q), ΔG = −nFE, Ellingham diagram (`ellingham`), Al electrolysis voltage.
9. Haber–Bosch: equilibrium from NIST Shomate data (`ammoniaEq`), Temkin–Pyzhev rate in a plug-flow bed (`converter`).
10. Regular solution, common tangent, binodal and spinodal (`demix`).
11. Frontier as of November 2026: established / demonstrated / claimed; minimum-work yardstick (`minWorkCO2`,
    `kWhPerKgH2`).
12. Closing: four questions.

Diagrams (9, generated by tools/diagrams.cjs, each wide + narrow): ledger, count (to scale), bath, quad, mu, barrier,
couple (to scale, from models), loop, volt (to scale, from models).
