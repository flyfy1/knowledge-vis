## The one question

每个月的房贷，钱到底还到哪儿去了？ The answer the essay builds from one rule, *this month's interest = monthly rate × what you still owe*: part of each payment is the price of the balance outstanding that month, the rest reduces it. Every later idea (front-loaded interest, equal-principal vs equal-payment, prepaying, rate resets, flat-fee instalments, inflation) is that rule applied to a different schedule of balances.

## Changes from the planning list

- Order follows the rule rather than the list: rates (ch1) → payment (ch2) → schedule (ch3) → two repayment methods (ch4) → prepaying (ch5) → rate change (ch6) → IRR (ch7) → inflation/opportunity cost (ch8) → method (ch9).
- Compound interest itself is not re-taught: ch1 links to `investment/rates/compound-interest/#step1` and only adds the three names of one rate. Ch3 links to `compound-interest#step5` and `where-money-comes-from#ch7` (same 100万/4%/30-year loan there) and adds the crossover month and why it sits there.
- The "honest comparison" in ch4 and ch5 is made exact by the present-value reading of ch2: at the loan rate both repayment methods are worth exactly P, and both prepayment modes remove exactly X. Interest differences are then shown to equal the difference in balance-months (interest = i × Σ balances, pinned at every slider extreme).
- Ch8 combines inflation and opportunity cost in one model and finds a result worth stating: the prepay-vs-alternative comparison breaks even exactly at the loan rate whatever the inflation, because inflation divides both sides by the same factor.
- The planned "APR nearly double" result is reproduced but qualified by the model: the ratio is 1.50 at 3 instalments, 1.81 at 12, 1.84 at 24 and falls back at 36 (as n grows, compounding pulls the IRR toward the flat fee), so the page says "接近两倍" only as the rough reading for short plans.

## Conventions (all chapters)

Nominal annual rate r compounded monthly, i = r/12; interest = opening balance × i; payments at month end; no rounding to cents, no day counts; no fees except ch7. Base loan: P = 1,000,000, r = 4%, n = 360 (illustrative round numbers, stated as such on the page).

## Chapters

Each: law · everyday case · model (state; reader controls; what to discover; standard result) · limits.

1. **合同上的利率，和每个月用的利率** (`#ch1`). Law: the bank uses r/12 each month; the effective rate is higher. Case: a year with no payments. Model `staircase/effective`: owed after k periods P(1 + r/m)^k vs the simple line. Controls: r 1–36%, m ∈ {1, 4, 12, 365}. Discover: 4% monthly → 0.333%/month, 4.07% a year (104.07万 owed); 24% → 26.82%; daily 4.08% (bounded by e^r − 1). Standard result: effective annual rate (1 + r/m)^m − 1. Limits: equal months; some lenders count days.
2. **月供是怎么定出来的** (`#ch2`). Law: the payment is the unique level amount that clears the balance in month n. Case: guess the payment. Model `simulateFixed/payoffMonth/payment`: raw month loop B ← B(1+i) − M. Control: guess 3000–7000 (step 10) and a formula button (exact 4774.15). Discover: ≤3333 never falls (interest 3333); 4000 leaves 53.7万 after 30 years and takes 44 y 11 m; ≈4774 is exact. Standard result: annuity formula M = P·i/(1 − (1+i)^−n) and its present-value reading. Animation: the path draws at 120 months/s on real dt, only on screen; complete at once under reduced motion. Limits: rounding to cents, first-period day count.
3. **前几年为什么主要在还利息** (`#ch3`). Law: interest is on what is owed, so early payments are mostly interest. Case: "I paid for years and the balance barely moved". Model `amortise`: rows of interest/principal. Controls: r 1–8%, term 10–30 y, month cursor. Discover: first month 3333 interest / 1441 principal; principal part grows by (1+i) a month; crossover month 153 (year 13); first 10 years 57.3万 paid, 21.2万 principal; total interest 71.9万; 3% → month 84; 2% → month 1; 20 years → 6060/month, crossover 33, 45.4万. Limits: fixed rate, no fees.
4. **等额本金和等额本息，差在哪儿** (`#ch4`). Law: same rate; equal-principal costs less only because less is owed for less time. Model `equalPrincipal` vs `amortise`, shaded area between balance curves. Controls: r, term. Discover: 6111 vs 4774 first month; EP below annuity from month 146; last EP 2787; interest 60.2万 vs 71.9万 (−11.7万); average balance 59.9万 vs 50.1万; interest ratio = balance ratio exactly; PV at loan rate of both = 100万. Standard result: total EP interest P·i·(n+1)/2; present value. Limits: same rate and no fees assumed.
5. **提前还一笔钱** (`#ch5`). Law: prepaid money stops bearing interest; savings depend on how long it would have been owed. Model `prepay/savingCurve/pvFrom`. Controls: X 1–50万, year-end 1–29, mode term/payment. Discover: 10万 at year 5 → term: 4 y 4 m sooner, 14.9万 saved; payment: 4774 → 4246, 5.8万 saved; both reduce the PV of remaining payments by exactly 10万; same 10万 saves 18.8万 at year 1, 4.2万 at year 20. Limits: penalties, minimums and whether prepaying is allowed are not modelled; no advice.
6. **利率变了以后** (`#ch6`). Law: a rate change changes every later month's interest; how the payment follows depends on the contract. Model `rateChange` (one reset after month k; re-amortise over n − k, or keep payment). Controls: reset year 1–29, Δ −3…+3 pp, mode. Discover: +1 pp at year 3 → owed 94.5万, payment 5321, +17.7万 interest; keep mode → 7 y 11 m longer, +45.3万; negative amortisation threshold 6.06% (+2.06 pp) at year 3. Limits: one change, immediate; no forecasts.
7. **真实的年利率** (`#ch7`). Law: the fair comparison is the rate that equates money received and paid at their dates. Case: 12,000 in 12 instalments at 0.6%/month fee on the original principal. Model `flatPlan/irr/npv` (bisection on a bracket, NPV plotted against the trial rate). Controls: fee 0.2–1.5%, up-front fee 0–5%, n ∈ {3, 6, 12, 24, 36}. Discover: 1072/month, 864 fees, "7.2%"; average owed 6500; IRR 1.086%/month → 13.0% (×12), 13.8% effective, 1.81×; 1.50× at 3, 1.84× at 24, lower at 36. Mortgage cash flows through the same solver give exactly 4%; a 1% up-front fee → 4.08% over 30 years, 4.23% if repaid after 5. Standard result: internal rate of return. Limits: unique root for one inflow then outflows; no penalties, no cash-discount cost.
8. **30 年后的 4774 元** (`#ch8`). Law: inflation lightens a fixed payment but does not change whether prepaying beats another use; the yardstick is the loan rate. Model `realValue/realTotals/prepayOrInvest`: A = prepay 10万 at year 5 (term mode) and invest the freed payments at g until month 360; B = invest 10万 at g from month 60; compare at month 360, deflated. Controls: π 0–8%, g 0–8%. Discover: at 2% the last payment is worth 2636 today; 171.9万 nominal = 129.5万 today; if π = 4.07% (the effective loan rate) real total = exactly 100万; break-even exactly at g = 4%; at g = 0 the gap equals ch5's 14.9万 saved interest. Standard results: deflating by a price index, Fisher relation (1+EAR)/(1+π) − 1, future value. Limits: fixed inflation, no income growth, no tax, the alternative return taken as certain (stated as a model, not advice).
9. **拿到一份贷款合同时，你能自己算的** (`#ch9`). Three questions, four things the reader can now compute, a not-advice paragraph, links.

## Number formats

Chinese: sums ≥ 10,000 in 万 (`u.wan`), monthly amounts in whole yuan up to 100,000; English: full amounts with digit grouping as ¥1,000,000 (`u.wan` is never used in English), negative amounts as −¥29,825. Money axes: 万 in Chinese, thousands in English ("¥ thousands"). Durations: `u.dur.y`/`u.dur.y1`/`u.dur.m`/`u.dur.m1` and `u.dur.ym` = "{Y} {M}", so English has "1 year 1 month"; `u.pp1` for ±1 point; `u.yearOut` for slider outputs. Rates on sliders drop trailing zeros.
