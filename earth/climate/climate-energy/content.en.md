Step 0 · Latitude & input {#s0k .step-kicker i18n=text}

Incoming solar power is approximately **solar constant × projected area**. At higher latitude the rays arrive at a shallower angle, spreading the same energy over more ground. {#s0e .explain}

## Bright surfaces send light back {#step1 h2=s1t}

::: strings

Step 1 · Albedo {#s1k .step-kicker i18n=text}

Albedo *α* is the fraction reflected. Snow, cloud and desert have high α; forest and ocean are lower. The remaining **1 − α** enters the surface–atmosphere system. {#s1e .explain}

## Escaping heat sets the balance temperature {#step2 h2=s2t}

::: strings
nav2: 2 Greenhouse effect

Step 2 · Greenhouse effect {#s2k .step-kicker i18n=text}

After absorbing shortwave light, the surface cools by infrared radiation. Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit some of that infrared; the system warms until **outgoing energy = absorbed energy**. {#s2e .explain}

::: html step2-1

:::

This is a radiative-balance teaching model: it shows direction and feedback, not clouds, oceans or circulation in a full climate model. {#s2h .hint i18n=text}
