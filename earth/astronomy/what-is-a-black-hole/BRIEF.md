## The one question

黑洞到底是什么？ What is a black hole, really?

Answer the essay builds: a mass M that has fallen entirely inside r_s = 2GM/c². Far away it is just that mass (ch3); within a few r_s orbits stop closing and below 3 r_s no circular orbit is stable (ch4), clocks slow (ch6), light is captured inside b = 2.6 r_s (ch7). Because r_s ∝ M, everything scales with mass: tides tear you apart outside small holes and are gentle at large ones (ch2, ch5). The evidence is three independent lines: stellar orbits (ch8), the shadow size (ch7), merger waves (ch9). Ch10 gives three questions to ask of any black-hole headline and states what the page does not cover.

## Chapters

Each: law · everyday case · model (state, parameters, reader controls, discovery, closed form) · limits.

1. **连光都追不上的逃逸速度** (`#ch1`). Law: squeeze a fixed mass and the escape speed grows until it equals c. Case: throwing a stone; 11.2 km/s. Model: `v = √(2GM/R)`, R = R₀·(r_s/R₀)^p with the reader's "squeeze" p ∈ [0, 1]; objects Earth, Sun, Sgr A* (R₀ = S2's pericentre, 119 AU). Log–log chart, line slope −½, meets c at r_s. Discover: r_s Earth 8.87 mm (radius ÷ 7.2 × 10⁸), Sun 2.95 km (÷ 24 万), Sgr A* 1.27 × 10⁷ km = 18 R☉. Prose states explicitly that this Newtonian argument is a heuristic and that it lands on 2GM/c² by coincidence; the horizon comes from Schwarzschild's solution. Limits: mass fixed while squeezing; formation not covered.
2. **黑洞有多大，只看它有多重** (`#ch2`). Law: r_s ∝ M, so mass ÷ volume ∝ M⁻². Model: exact; mass slider 10⁻⁶–10^10.5 M☉ and presets (Earth, Sun, GW150914 remnant 62 M☉, Sgr A*, M87*). Discover: 1 M☉ 1.8 × 10¹⁹ kg/m³; Sgr A* ≈ 1.0 × 10⁶ (≈ 1000× water); below water above 1.4 × 10⁸ M☉; M87* r_s 128 AU, 0.44 kg/m³. Limits: "mass ÷ volume" is bookkeeping only; nothing said about the interior.
3. **黑洞不会“吸”** (`#ch3`). Law: same distance, same pull; mass changes orbits, compactness does not. Model: velocity-Verlet planet on a 1 AU circle (live, 0.4 yr/s, h = 0.0005 yr); "swap Sun for a black hole" changes only the drawing (the force law is identical, shell theorem); mass slider 0.25–2 M☉ resets the planet to the circle and changes the mass at that instant; exact Kepler elements for the readout. Discover: year 365.3 d unchanged on the swap; 2 M☉ → perihelion 0.33 AU, 140.6 d; 0.5 M☉ → exactly parabolic (v_circ·√2 = v_esc). Limits: Newtonian; sudden mass change is a thought experiment.
4. **靠得太近，牛顿就不够用了** (`#ch4`). Law: within a few r_s gravity is stronger than Newton's; orbits precess; no stable circular orbit inside 3 r_s. Model: Schwarzschild timelike geodesics in proper time, r_s = c = 1, `r̈ = −GM/r² + h²/r³ − 3GMh²/r⁴` (RK4, H = 0.02), Newton drops the last term; effective potential plotted with the energy line; fate (bound / plunge / escape) by scanning V(r) = E; precession from the Binet equation `u'' + u = GM/h² + 3GMu²` between turning points (agrees with the time-stepped simulation to < 0.1°, and with 6πGM/(c²a(1−e²)) for wide orbits to < 1 %). Reader: start radius 4–20 r_s, tangential speed 0.5–1.3 × Newtonian circular, GR/Newton, replay. Discover: default (10 r_s, 0.9) GR pericentre 4.23 r_s, 153.4°/orbit, Newton 6.81 closed; 0.8 plunges in GR, Newton 4.71; ISCO 3 r_s; photon sphere 1.5 r_s. Limits: proper time; non-rotating; ISCO shifts with spin (not computed).
5. **被拉成面条，还是平安穿过** (`#ch5`). Law: what tears you is the difference in pull, which at the horizon ∝ M⁻². Model: radial tidal stretch `Δg = 2GML/r³`, L = 2 m (in GR this is exactly the radial tidal acceleration for a radially free-falling observer, in Schwarzschild r; transverse squeeze is half: Misner, Thorne & Wheeler, *Gravitation*, §31.2 — textbook result, not re-opened). Reader: mass 1–10¹⁰ M☉, distance 1–1000 r_s. Discover: Earth across you 6.3 × 10⁻⁷ g; 10 M☉ horizon 2.1 × 10⁷ g, 1 g reached at 8150 km = 276 r_s; Sgr A* horizon 1.1 × 10⁻⁴ g; dividing mass (1 g at the horizon) 4.6 × 10⁴ M☉. Limits: body as a rod; "torn apart" threshold (1 g, i.e. stretch equals body weight) is a convention, order of magnitude only.
6. **视界附近，时间走得慢** (`#ch6`). Law: a hovering clock ticks at √(1 − r_s/r). Model: two dials driven by real dt (far clock 10 s per real s, near clock × rate); rate curve vs log(r/r_s − 1); hover acceleration `GM/(r²√(1−r_s/r))` for 10 M☉ or Sgr A*. Discover: 1.5 r_s → 0.577; 1.01 r_s → 0.0995 (1 h ↔ ≈ 10 h); Sgr A* hover at 1.01 r_s 3.6 × 10⁶ g; Earth surface 7.0 × 10⁻¹⁰; GPS (a = 26 561.75 km) +45.7 μs/day gravitational, −7.2 μs/day speed (special relativity), net ≈ 38. Limits: static observers only; Earth rotation and orbit eccentricity ignored.
7. **黑洞的影子比它大** (`#ch7`). Law: light aimed within 2.6 r_s is captured, so the shadow is 2.6× the horizon. Model: null geodesics `u'' + u = 3GMu²` (RK4 in φ, step 0.002, exit interpolated), fan of 15 rays plus the reader's ray (b 0–12 r_s), a photon dot moving on real dt, and the camera view with the dark disc of radius b_c = √27·GM/c² = 2.598 r_s. Discover: b = 3 → 98.5°; capture switches between 2.59 and 2.61; weak field → 2 r_s/b. EHT: predicted Sgr A* shadow 53 μas from GRAVITY 2022 mass and distance vs ring 51.8 ± 2.3 and inferred shadow 48.7 ± 7.0 μas (test checks the prediction lies inside the latter). Limits: one plane; no emission model; spin can shrink the shadow by up to ~8 % (EHT 2022).
8. **一颗恒星称出了银河系中心的质量** (`#ch8`). Law: period + orbit size → central mass by Kepler III; then ask what fits inside. Model: Kepler orbit solved from Kepler's equation each frame (1.6 yr/s), a = α·D with α = 125.058 mas; reader drags D 7500–9000 pc. Discover: at 8246.7 pc a = 1031 AU, M = 4.26 × 10⁶ M☉ (equals GRAVITY's fitted 4.261), pericentre 119 AU = 1414 r_s, 7740 km/s = 2.6 % c; D +10 % → M +33 %; weak-field precession 12.2′/orbit vs measured ≈ 12′. Confidence tag 较有把握 on "it is a black hole". Limits: two-body Kepler; inference, not a direct view of the horizon. Uses the GRAVITY 2020 mass (4.26) here and the GRAVITY 2022 mass (4.297, via EHT 2022) elsewhere; both are stated where used.
9. **两个黑洞相撞，时空在颤动** (`#ch9`). Law: orbiting black holes radiate, spiral in and chirp. Model: leading-order quadrupole chirp `f(τ) = (1/π)(5/256τ)^{3/8}(GM_c/c³)^{−5/8}`, exact phase, waveform ∝ f^{2/3}; stops at f_ISCO = c³/(6^{3/2}πGM) (ch4's 3 r_s). Reader: m₁, m₂ 5–80 M☉; slow-motion playhead (×0.1). Discover: 36 + 29 → M_c 28.1, 35 → 68 Hz in 0.16 s, 7.1 cycles, separation 576 km; real signal continues to 250 Hz (merger, not modelled); remnant 62 M☉ → r_s 183 km. "Too heavy" sentence when f_ISCO < 37 Hz. Limits: leading order, point masses, no cosmological redshift (z ≈ 0.09), no merger/ringdown.
10. **那么，黑洞到底是什么** (`#ch10`). No model. Three questions (how heavy? how far away? measured or inferred?), four things not covered with labels: interior/singularity (尚无答案), spin and charge, Hawking radiation (理论预言，未观测到; T = ħc³/(8πGMk_B): 1 M☉ 6.2 × 10⁻⁸ K, Sgr A* 1.4 × 10⁻¹⁴ K, hotter than the CMB only below 4.5 × 10²² kg = 0.75 % of Earth's mass), formation.

Reuse of earlier models by name: ch2 (ch1's r_s), ch3 (ch1's escape speed), ch5 (ch2's r_s ∝ M), ch7 (ch4's photon sphere), ch8 (ch3's Kepler, ch4's precession, ch6's redshift), ch9 (ch4's ISCO), ch10 (all).

## Changes from the brief's starting list

- Added ch2 (size and "density" ∝ M⁻²) because the surprising result (big holes thinner than water) prepares ch5's "bigger is gentler".
- Added ch4 (relativistic orbits, ISCO, photon sphere) because the brief's chapters needed it twice: the photon sphere explains the 2.6 r_s shadow (ch7) and the ISCO sets where the chirp formula stops (ch9). It is also the honest answer to "where does a black hole stop behaving like any other mass?".
- Split the evidence into three chapters (shadow in ch7 with its model, S2 in ch8, gravitational waves in ch9), each with a model.
- Hawking radiation only in ch10, with its scale computed.
