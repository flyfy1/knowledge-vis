# Climate Feedback · 教学 BRIEF

## Learning outcome
Learners can trace the causal chain from emissions and atmospheric composition to radiative forcing, surface adjustment, ice/albedo and water-vapour feedback, then distinguish atmospheric accumulation from ocean/land uptake.

## Interaction contract
- Step 0 sliders update a canvas energy ledger, forcing, absorbed sunlight and model surface temperature.
- Step 1 emissions and uptake sliders update a three-destination carbon budget with atmospheric ppm/year.
- Step 2 feedback cards toggle ice, water-vapour and cloud links; the loop redraws with active/disabled arrows and a gain readout.
- Step 3 selects and checks a misconception claim; only the complete causal chain passes.
- Chinese/English toggle preserves the current controls and feedback selection.

## Scope and model limits
The canvas is a schematic, not a forecast. The climate equations are deliberately relative teaching quantities: logarithmic CO₂ forcing, albedo forcing, and simple feedback multipliers. Clouds are shown as an uncertain pathway rather than assigned a single sign.
