## Which three features do plant cells add? {#step3 h2=s3t}

::: strings
nav3: 3 Animal vs plant
:::

STEP 3 · ANIMAL VS PLANT {#s3k .step-kicker i18n=text}

This step answers: which structures differ between animal and plant cells? Switch to the plant cell, then click the three extra features in the diagram or the buttons below (shared structures are mixed in; a wrong pick tells you why). A **cell wall** supports the cell, a **chloroplast** runs photosynthesis, and a **large vacuole** stores water and materials; shared structures keep the same colour. {#s3e .explain}

::: strings
