## Correctness bridge
A shortest enclosing fence is convex: an inward detour is longer than its straight chord and cannot be necessary to enclose trees. Its boundary edges have every tree on one side. Exhaustive pair testing is a correct independent baseline. Sorting makes each new point the next extreme in scan direction. On a negative turn A,B,C, B lies above chord AC in a lower scan, so B cannot remain on that lower boundary; later extension cannot restore it as lower boundary. Repeatedly remove the last inward bend; earlier bends are unchanged. A nonnegative turn preserves the convex lower chain of the processed prefix, including points on its straight edges. Reverse scan covers the upper boundary, including vertical extremes. Two chains cover the convex enclosure; unique union keeps every boundary point once. Singleton and all-collinear cases require no polygon-area assumptions.
