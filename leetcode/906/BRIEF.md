## Proof / cost / state
Each positive palindromic root has one length parity and one positive leading half; reconstruction gives exactly that root and no other parity has its length. Increasing seeds give increasing roots within each parity (including length boundaries), so break after root>isqrt(right) is sound. Positive squares are injective, giving no duplicate answer. Test both inclusive bounds and square reversal. D=O(log R) digits, O(R^1/4) seeds, O(R^1/4 log R) time, O(log R) temporary space; exact integer arithmetic, with ordinary bounded-size arithmetic convention (official bound at most18 digits).
