## Proof / baseline / simulation boundaries
Baseline enumerates 2^n subsets and checks n nodes O(n2^n), O(n) space. Independent oracle uses undirected adjacency and subset coverage, not greedy states. Greedy exchange: on residual uncovered demands, a lowest uncovered node can be covered at itself, a child, or its parent; shifting that not-yet-fixed camera to the parent loses no pending lower demand, while fixed cameras preserve resolved demands and may cover sibling/ancestor. Freeze that choice, remove covered demands and repeat. Child summaries retain exactly obligations that cross a parent edge. N child forces parent camera in a transformed optimum; K child covers parent; otherwise defer parent's own demand. Root N cannot defer. Canonical iterative stack and dict are O(n) space, not O(h).
