Proof: any duplicate x is the prefix of two suffixes; all suffixes beginning x form a contiguous sorted block, so two neighbors in that block share x. Neighbor prefixes are themselves valid repeats: their maximum is exactly optimum. Rank invariant: rank[i] orders s[i:i+k], including shorter suffixes. Pair (rank[i],rank[i+k] or -1) orders two halves, therefore 2k characters; compress equal pairs, double k. Unique ranks imply full suffix order because every pair has already differed within known prefixes. At most ceil(log2 n) rounds; key sort O(n log n) per round, O(n) keys/ranks. Avoid full suffix slices in canonical sorting. LCP uses original i order, predecessor j. If preceding LCP is h, removing first characters leaves h-1 equal; shifted predecessor is below current suffix, so actual predecessor lies between and shares at least h-1. Rank0 has no predecessor and resets h. Each successful extension increases h, each i lowers it at most one; reset at rank0 occurs once, so total successful extensions O(n), plus at most one failing comparison per i. Maximum LCP's substring is output once. Baseline pairs all starts with direct character comparison O(n³) time,O(n) answer storage; n=30000 gives 449985000 pairs before character scans.
