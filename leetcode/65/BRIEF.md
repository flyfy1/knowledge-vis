## New terms at first use (also redefined locally)
Step0: digit = ASCII 0–9; mantissa = number before optional exponent; exponent = e/E followed by signed integer. Step1: prefix = consumed left part; invariant = fact preserved after each legal character; digit = seen digit in current mantissa/exponent part, dot/exp = punctuation already seen, dead = no suffix can repair. Step2 repeats prefix, character classes, flags, acceptance. Step3 repeats current-part digit requirement. Step4 repeats flags/sign guard and complexity n=input length. No terms rely on another step being read.

## Reasoning and proof obligations
Split-grammar baseline checks optional exponent and mantissa pieces independently, O(n) time/O(n) copied text; no invented asymptotic time gain. Streaming flags avoid slices and keep O(1) auxiliary memory. Induct on consumed prefix: a digit satisfies current part; one dot before e leaves digit unchanged; e requires completed mantissa then clears digit; sign only initially/immediately after e; all other events reject forever. At end digit true is necessary and sufficient. `1e+` refutes 'one digit anywhere is enough'; `99e2.5` refutes treating exponent as another decimal; conversion accepts nan/inf and has magnitude semantics, so not the grammar oracle.
