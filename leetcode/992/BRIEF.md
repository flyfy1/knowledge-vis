## Terms and proof dependency
Step0 subarray=contiguous nonempty interval; distinct=different values; l,r zero-based inclusive endpoints. Step1 baseline=correct slow enumeration; atMost(t)=number of intervals with <=t values; set records unique seen values. Step2 window=[left,r], frequency=value multiplicity, dictionary keys=positive-frequency values, left_t=earliest valid start. Append can increase distinct count by at most one; removing left elements never increases it. All earlier invalid starts remain invalid after appending; shrink stops at first valid start, so precisely left_t..r qualify. For threshold0 left=r+1 gives an empty window and zero nonempty intervals. AtMost(k-1) subset of AtMost(k), so subtraction retains exactly k. Per endpoint exact=left_(k-1)−left_k. Summing partitions intervals by unique right endpoint. Two scans each advance right n times and left at most n times; dictionary average O(1) gives expected O(n) time, O(n) space. Step3 locally restates two boundaries; step4 defines helper APIs and deletion/zero count.
