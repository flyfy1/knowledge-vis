## Source → claim → model

[OpenStax16.5](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-5-interference-of-waves) defines linear same-point superposition and phase-dependent resultant amplitude. [OpenStax16.4](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/16-4-energy-and-power-of-a-wave) distinguishes displacement amplitude from energy transport and conditional amplitude-squared power scaling. Both read; no copied figures or measured data.

Directed d=r2−r1, arrival phase δ=φ−kd. For equal received amplitudes and same fixed frequency, B=2A|cos(δ/2)|. Canonical phase classification and B share one helper, with only machine-scale snapping near integer/half-integer cycles; not a physical tolerance or measurement. Earlier in-phase lessons may display |d|, but S3 formula/readout uses directed d. Near-zero noncanonical B uses scientific notation, never rounds to a falsely exact0. Source-phase coefficient must retain the native .005π step: one ArrowLeft fromπ shows0.995π, not1π; arrival-phase display is explicitly approximate. This was an actual cold-trial recurrence fix, not just cosmetic precision. General states are not called full reinforcement or cancellation. Actual yAt/ySum remain original functions; numerical orbit checks compare B to independent Fourier/quadrature of ySum, signed positions, periodicity and limits.

Coherence means same frequency and fixed relative phase during each run. Changing φ is a paused hypothetical comparison, not a real instantaneous phase-switch transient. A1 and coordinate lengths are normalized teaching values. Equal amplitude at P, no attenuation/spreading/reflections/dispersion modeled; rings are phase-position illustration, not a fluid/energy solver. Interference does not destroy energy; the page computes displacement, not energy flux. Do not label2A as twice intensity. An amplitude-squared intensity relation requires the appropriate wave/medium/frequency assumptions and is not used as a numeric energy map for these2D rings.
