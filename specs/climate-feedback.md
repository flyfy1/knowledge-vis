# Climate Feedback visual spec

- **Slug:** `climate-feedback`
- **Domain:** Earth & Space / senior high
- **Core question:** How does a composition change become a temperature change, and why can a small initial forcing propagate through feedbacks?
- **Causal chain:** emissions → atmospheric CO₂ → infrared forcing → temperature adjustment → ice/albedo + water-vapour feedback → top-of-atmosphere balance.
- **Controls:** CO₂ (280–800 ppm), planetary albedo (0.20–0.40), water feedback (0–100%), emissions (0–60 GtC/yr), uptake (0–90%), feedback toggles.
- **Misconception check:** small concentration means no effect; uptake means disappearance; unchanged Sun means unchanged surface temperature.
- **Validation:** `node climate-feedback/tests/core.cjs`.
