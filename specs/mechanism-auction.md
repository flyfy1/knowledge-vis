# Mechanism Design & Auctions

## Route
- slug: `mechanism-auction`
- category: `math`

## Causal contract
1. Separate private value from submitted bid.
2. First-price auctions charge the winner their own bid; second-price auctions charge the runner-up bid.
3. Utility is value minus payment for the winner and zero for the loser.
4. Scope is two bidders, one item and independent private values; no legal or financial advice.
