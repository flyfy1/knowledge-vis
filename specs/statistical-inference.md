# Statistical Inference

## Route
- slug: `statistical-inference`
- category: `probability`
- stage: `senior`

## Causal contract
1. Sampling means vary around μ with `SE = σ/√n`.
2. A confidence interval is `x̄ ± z·SE`; confidence and sample size move its width in different directions.
3. A two-sided p-value is computed under the null from `z = (x̄−μ₀)/SE`; it is not the null probability or effect size.

## Scope
Normal-theory teaching model with known σ. It does not replace study design, domain expertise or scientific decision making.
