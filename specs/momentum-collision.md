## Causal contract
1. `p = m·v`: mass and signed velocity update the momentum arrow and readout.
2. `J = F·Δt = Δp`: force and duration update the impulse and change in momentum together.
3. Elastic collision preserves total momentum and kinetic energy; sticky collision preserves total momentum and merges velocities.
4. For a fixed momentum change, `|F| = |Δp|/Δt`; longer stopping time lowers average force.

## Scope
One-dimensional ideal bodies. Real collisions add rotation, friction and deformation.
