### Correctness, invariants and executable checks

Audited canonical solution.py, core.js, app.js, index.html, copy.js and styles. For each end r, starts l are visited 0…r. When a length ≥3 substring needs its interior, column r−1 is already complete; dp[l] is also complete because l≤r. Endpoint equality plus an empty/singleton/palindromic interior is necessary and sufficient. Every nonempty partition has a final piece, so minimizing over all legal final pieces neither loses a solution nor invents one. Singletons ensure at least one candidate; the last column supplies the final answer. Zero-initialized bytearray false values correspond to tested F; the simulation's null/unknown cells intentionally distinguish not-yet-tested entries and are labeled accordingly.

At each intermediate candidate, the current dp is the minimum over legal starts tested so far, or n+1 if none. Completed columns are optimal prefixes. Rejected pieces leave the sentinel/current minimum unchanged; legal but worse pieces also leave it unchanged. The aab third candidate explicitly rejects a proposed increase from 1 to 2. The final candidate maps to the canonical `palindrome[2][2]=1` and `pieces[3]=min(4,pieces[2]+1)=2`. After subtracting one, answer=1. Highlighting marks the latest comparison; T/F and stored numeric values remain visible as data.
