## Required finding

**233-I1 (open, required; interaction/visual meaning).** In `leetcode/233/app.js`, selecting a transfer answer immediately calls `transferRender()`, which unhides the feedback and fills the contribution table; `#transferReveal` has no click handler. Reproduction: at any locale/width, select `choice23` in Step 4 before clicking “Reveal answer”; feedback already reads “Correct…” and rows are already visible, and clicking the reveal button changes nothing. Expected: the prediction remains unrevealed until the explicit reveal action, then the selected prediction is evaluated and rows appear. Actual: answer/reasoning is revealed on selection, so the required predict→reveal sequence and distinction between data and highlighting are broken. This violates the rubric’s interaction/visual-meaning requirement and the page’s own “Choose first. After reveal…” instruction. Fix the state machine and re-review Step 4 at both locales and supported widths.
