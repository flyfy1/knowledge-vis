Querying before inserting current P forbids empty intervals. Any interval ending here subtracts one earlier prefix; the inequality excludes q<P−k, and minimizing eligible q maximizes the result. No eligible q leaves best unchanged. Duplicate equal prefixes remain legitimate occurrences.
