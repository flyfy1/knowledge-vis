## Behavior and acceptance criteria

1. Buck and Boost have a complete, compact SVG circuit at 320px. Source, switch, diode polarity, inductor, capacitor terminals, load and return wires remain recognizable. No horizontal circuit scrolling is needed. Do not restore a desktop-only 720px minimum circuit width.
2. Bright conductor width encodes absolute conventional current on a fixed scale. Static arrows communicate direction without color or motion alone. Dashes move only on conducting branches, with forward displacement integrated from elapsed display time and current magnitude. Never multiply total elapsed time by instantaneous current: falling current can then visually reverse flow. Pausing freezes the view, not the physical current.
3. All current/state/energy values derive from unchanged `PowerModel.converter(...).sample(t)`. Buck source disconnects in OFF and the diode closes the return loop. Boost ON blocks delivery to the output, while the capacitor supplies the load through its separate closed loop; OFF retains source delivery while magnetic energy falls. Output-node KCL is `delivery = 1 A + iC`.
4. Capacitor terminal paths reverse according to signed iC, independently of ON/OFF. Zero iC has no active capacitor flow. No dashes cross the dielectric gap. Capacitor voltage/energy ripple is not simulated. Inductor field emphasis and the fixed-scale meter encode `E = ½Li²`; increasing/decreasing wording follows `vL × iL`. Current dashes do not represent energy packets or electron velocity.
5. Play starts paused, runs at approximately four display seconds per physical 50µs cycle, and stops after two cycles. Play, Pause, Replay, Next, Reset and the time slider share a single time coordinate. At most one converter runs. Reset stops playback and restores t=0. Hidden documents, offscreen circuits, language changes and motion-preference changes pause; no background catch-up. Starting playback brings the circuit into view on mobile.
6. Vin 5–12V and D 20–75% controls stay adjacent to the circuit. Parameter changes preserve inspection time and recompute a new periodic steady case; the page explicitly discloses that this is not an adjustment transient. Holding t=20µs while moving D from 20% to 75% must visibly change OFF to ON. The ON-duration bar changes geometrically with D, and the cursor follows physical phase.
7. Traces show the analytic trajectory through inspected time and are recomputed on edits. Include exact switch and cycle boundaries so the voltage trace does not draw a false ramp across a discontinuity. Current stays continuous. Next retains the original unequal ON/OFF half-phase sampling contract.
8. Default is no autoplay. Reduced motion hides all moving dashes while manual state/time inspection and explicit slow playback remain usable. Native sliders/buttons, 48px controls, 3px focus, textual states and forced colors are preserved. No per-frame live announcements. The SVG circuit node survives updates. One RAF, bounded paths and ~12.5Hz paint cap; no external assets or new dependencies.
9. Visible Codex attribution is owned by this page. Future Claude comparisons should use the same physics domain, held-time parameter task, mobile widths, reduced-motion behavior and tests; don't compare an expanded quantitative solver with this bounded presentation change.
