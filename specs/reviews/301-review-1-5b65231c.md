## Cold walkthrough (recorded before source/tests/author material)

I opened the candidate in a fresh browser at 1280×900 and read the page as a new reader. The page exposed five numbered teaching steps.

1. **01 · Count unavoidable deletions**
   - **Question:** What is the task and why is a deletion unavoidable in `()())()`?
   - **Action:** Read the introductory worked scan and the displayed balances.
   - **Observable result:** The page shows balances `0→1→0→1→0→-1`, says the final `)` makes a prefix invalid, and shows two one-deletion outputs `(())()` and `()()()`.
   - **Causal paraphrase:** A negative prefix cannot be repaired later, so at least one right parenthesis must be removed; different positions can produce distinct shortest answers.

2. **02 · Why the budget is enough**
   - **Question:** Why is a fixed greedy deletion insufficient, and what does the budget buy?
   - **Action:** Read the explanation of `left_rem/right_rem` and the subset oracle.
   - **Observable result:** It says deleting the second or fifth `)` is optimal and that the oracle keeps longest unique valid strings, while the submission deletes exactly the computed left/right counts and prunes negative prefixes.
   - **Causal paraphrase:** Counting both unmatched directions fixes the minimum deletion budget; search must retain alternative deletion positions to enumerate every shortest result.

3. **03 · Watch balance and pruning**
   - **Question:** What state must remain true while exploring a branch?
   - **Action:** Read the preset `()())()` at frame 0, click `Next frame →` twice, then click `← Back` once.
   - **Observable result:** Frame 0 shows `path = ∅; balance = 0; l=0, r=1`; frame 1 remains at the start; frame 2 shows `path = (` and `balance = 1`; Back returns to frame 1. The table records the kept path and balance/budgets, and the scrubber advertises revisiting viewed frames.
   - **Causal paraphrase:** Keeping `(` increases unmatched-open balance and consumes no deletion budget; the frame history can be advanced and rewound while preserving the selected state.

4. **04 · Predict before revealing**
   - **Question:** What is the shortest-result set for `((a)())`?
   - **Action:** Before reveal, I predicted option A (`["(a())","((a))"]`) as a deliberately independent wrong prediction, then selected A and clicked `Check and reveal`.
   - **Observable result:** The widget says **“Correct.”** It then claims: “One unmatched left parenthesis remains, so one deletion is required. Removing the outer or inner left parenthesis yields `(a())` and `((a))`; both are shortest.”
   - **Causal paraphrase intended by the page:** It attempts to teach that an unmatched-left budget creates two equivalent one-deletion choices. **Observed problem:** `((a)())` has three `(` and three `)` and is already valid, so the correct shortest set is `["((a)())"]` with zero deletions. The reveal is therefore a contradictory causal conclusion, not merely a difficult wording choice.

5. **05 · Implement and justify**
