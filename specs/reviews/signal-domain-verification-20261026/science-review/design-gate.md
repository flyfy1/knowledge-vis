## P1: necessary minimal corrections

1. **Reachable moving-average amplitude disagreement.** Actual `maSim(3,π)` reports ≈2/3 although its own steady y samples and an independently summed 3-tap FIR both have amplitude1/3. At degenerate cosine/sine bases the fallback incorrectly assumes both basis energies are L/2. Normalize the surviving basis by its actual Scc/Sss, rather than multiplying both correlations by2/L. Keep ordinary full-rank fitting. Exact DC is useful as a pure-function boundary (currently reported2 rather than1), but is not a current user-reachable DC bug and does not justify a new control.
2. **Existing exact preset must fit the native control domain.** M2→first-zero sets the state toπ, beyond the old native max3.13; then M3 retainsπ and reaches item1. Keep the existing positive range **0.05..π**, set step=any, label ←/→0.005, Shift0.05, Home0.05, Endπ and handle those native keys explicitly. Keep exact presets and arbitrary supported state without extra grid snapping; synchronize actual state/readout/native control. No extension to DC is needed.
3. **Zero formula has removable-limit exclusions.** The quotient's numerator zeros do not make k0 or k multiples of M filter zeros: at Ω=0 mod2π the gain is1. State zeros Ω=2πk/M for k not divisible byM (or, in the displayed 0..π interval, k1..floor(M/2)). Include/identify the legitimateπ endpoint for even M. At a true response zero, phase/local group delay is undefined; the familiar (M−1)/2 sample linear-phase delay describes nonzero-response bands, not an observed delay of a vanished sinusoid. A short qualifier is enough.
4. **Sampling uniqueness needs its signal class.** Above2f, arbitrary continuous signals still share all the samples: sin(2π·3n/25) and sin(2π·28n/25) agree. Replace “dots can only belong to the original” by the known band-limit/ideal sampling-and-reconstruction condition (B<fs/2). The finite displayed sample window and straight-line joining are illustrations, not a proof or exact sinc reconstruction.
5. **Equality does not become a general reconstruction guarantee through lucky phase.** At fs=2f, x[n]=(-1)^n A sinφ: φ0 disappears and φ90 alternates full scale, but that contrast does not prove general unique recovery. Even unit-amplitude φ30 and φ150 produce the same samples. Keep the useful phase control, remove “equality works if lucky”; explain loss of a quadrature/phase information at the boundary in plain language.
6. **Two box passes reduce aliasing, not guarantee its elimination.** Actual boxBlur twice on the existing period-four stripe pattern, r2 then stride3, retains an interior sampled range≈8.4 gray levels. Replace “no fake things remain” with reduction/residual and detail-loss language. 44.1kHz gives a20→22.05kHz transition margin, but a bandwidth inequality does not uniquely derive that historical standard; remove the sole-cause history claim rather than starting a history feature.
7. **Domain bridge, not an alleged whole-course domain defect.** Existing freqresp S1 rad/s, S3 rad/sample and S6 conversion are already correct. Add a concise bridge Ω=ωT=2πf/fs, T=1/fs, with units; do not globally rename or rewrite working models. Atlas impulse is explicitly δ[n]→h[n], so its frequency edge must identify the DTFT H(e^{iΩ})=Σh[n]e^{-iΩn}, distinct from continuous H(iω). Canonical frequency-response metadata can name both; the existing continuous pole/ROC relation remains continuous.
8. **Only Z5's linked unit/strip sentence enters this patch.** Sampling copies every fs inHz; Z5's equivalent separation is2π/T inrad/s. Give T seconds, ωrad/s and Ω=ωT. The principal strip is bounded in imaginary coordinate, e.g. −π/T<ω≤π/T, not a band centered about the imaginary axis. In the displayed σ<0 domain, images lie inside the unit circle; replace “whole z-plane” with one non-repeated angular turn. This is not a new Z-transform model audit.
