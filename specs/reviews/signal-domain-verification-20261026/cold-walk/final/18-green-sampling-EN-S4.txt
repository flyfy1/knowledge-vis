Step 4 · The frequency view

Aliasing in the frequency domain: the spectrum clones itself

A real sine has positive and negative frequency components. This diagram shows only positive-frequency peak positions, not a complete magnitude/phase spectrum. Ideal uniform sampling repeats the two-sided spectrum every fₛ Hz. With known B<fₛ/2, ideal reconstruction selects the two-sided baseband. The displayed [0,fₛ/2] is only its positive half; peak locations alone do not recover a waveform. Drag fₛ to see copies fold into this region. Notice what aliasing really is: not “the signal got a bit dirty”, but high-frequency information moved bodily into the low frequencies, impersonating them. Once sampling is done, the true peak and the alias peak look identical — no filter afterwards can separate them. Prevention has to happen before sampling.
