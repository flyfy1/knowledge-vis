# #964: statement and teaching preflight

[Official problem](https://leetcode.com/problems/least-operators-to-express-number/).
See [official response](964-official-access.json) and [dispatch prerequisites](README.md).
This is read-only preparation, not an implementation brief, candidate or acceptance.

**Verified contract.** `2 <= x <= 100`; `1 <= target <= 200000000`. Form a positive
target using repeated copies of x and binary addition, subtraction, multiplication
or rational division. Ordinary precedence applies; parentheses and unary negation
are forbidden. Return the minimum number of operator symbols. Python3 exposes
`Solution.leastOpsExpressTarget(self, x: int, target: int) -> int`; TypeScript exposes
`leastOpsExpressTarget(x: number, target: number): number`.

Official examples: `(3,19) -> 5`, `(5,501) -> 8`, `(100,100000000) -> 3`.

**Teaching pitfalls inferred from that contract:**

- Count operators, not occurrences of x. The expression consisting of x alone
  has zero operators; target 1 needs `x/x`. A displayed literal 1 or exponentiation
  is explanatory notation, not an allowed expression token.
- Rational division is not floor division. A verifier must preserve exact values;
  unrestricted binary-expression trees can accidentally introduce illegal grouping.
- A written negative term must follow a legal positive prefix with binary minus;
  do not silently allow a leading unary minus or parenthesized subtraction.
- Greedy choice of one nearby power is not a proof of optimality. Explain why
  overshooting and subtracting can compete with adding lower-power terms before
  introducing a carry/complement state; define both costs and their units.
- A power term's internal multiplications and the operator joining terms are
  different costs. If a recurrence charges every term a join, explain its final
  leading-join adjustment and the exceptional `x/x` unit cost. A small total
  should be checked against an actual legal expression with counted symbols.
- If a recurrence carries into a new power, explain termination and final carry
  handling. Do not confuse the large target bound with permission to enumerate
  every expression up to that value. Bound any teaching enumeration explicitly.

No expression generator, recurrence, optimization claim or exhaustive oracle has
been implemented or verified by this preparation. Those remain author/reviewer work.
