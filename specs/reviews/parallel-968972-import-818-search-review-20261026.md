## Race and failure limits

Polling does not deterministically prevent a very fast author from finishing
before STOP installation. This limitation is explicit: a completed/wrong-stage
run or premature reviewer causes an inspection-required failure, never a successful
handoff claim. The immediate recheck and final exact history check close false
success; they do not erase or interrupt a reviewer that won the race. Root must
inspect the actual receipt and zero-new-review evidence, not infer success merely
because a STOP exists. Such a failure requires a separately reviewed recovery.

Existing/foreign STOP content is not replaced. Failures preserve actual commits,
state and receipts, retaining/restoring the owned STOP if absent, and signal no
agent. A failed or blocked author cannot become a ready measurement candidate.
No blind retry, acceptance rollback or duplicate worker is allowed.
