## Causal contract
1. Working-memory load rises with item count; grouping reduces units to maintain.
2. Attention accuracy falls as distractors increase relative to targets.
3. Retrieval probability decays with delay and rises with a matching cue.
4. Old/new item counts combine into an interference-risk teaching metric.

## Scope
A bilingual teaching model for intuition. It makes no clinical, diagnostic, intelligence, or individual-treatment claim.
