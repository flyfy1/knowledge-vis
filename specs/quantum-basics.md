## Causal contract
1. A single-qubit state uses amplitudes `α = cos(θ/2)` and `β = sin(θ/2)`.
2. Measurement probabilities are squared amplitudes, `P(0)=|α|²`, `P(1)=|β|²`.
3. A reproducible seed samples one discrete result from a probability; it does not display a fractional outcome.
4. Phase changes interference by changing amplitude addition before squaring.

## Scope
Ideal single-qubit teaching model. It omits decoherence, noise, mixed states and multi-particle entanglement.
