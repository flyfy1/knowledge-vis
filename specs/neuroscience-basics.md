## Causal contract
1. Relative K⁺/Na⁺ leak shifts a simplified resting potential.
2. Below threshold, stimulus creates a graded potential; at threshold, state changes to a spike.
3. A refractory duration sets the compact maximum rate `1000 / refractory_ms`.

## Scope
A teaching model for one neuron. It does not claim a full Hodgkin–Huxley solver, synaptic network behavior, disease diagnosis or measured cell-specific values.
