## Causal contract
1. Stress–strain response begins with `σ = Eε` in the elastic region.
2. Yield strain separates reversible elastic storage from plastic deformation.
3. Cross-section changes total force through `F = σA` while the material curve remains a property model.
4. Toughness is the numerical area under stress–strain response up to fracture.

## Scope
Piecewise ideal curves for quasi-static uniaxial engineering stress–strain. Real behavior also depends on temperature, strain rate, defects, processing history and multiaxial stress.
