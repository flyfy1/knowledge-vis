# Game Theory

## Route
- slug: `game-theory`
- category: `math`
- stage: `university`

## Causal contract
1. A 2×2 payoff matrix lists consequences for player A and B in each simultaneous action pair.
2. Best responses compare one player’s payoffs while holding the opponent’s action fixed.
3. A Nash equilibrium is a cell where both players are best responding; a dominant strategy wins against every opponent move.
4. When no pure equilibrium exists, mixed probabilities make each player indifferent in a 2×2 model.

## Scope
Two players, two actions and one simultaneous round. Repeated games, bargaining, mechanism design and normative judgments are out of scope.
