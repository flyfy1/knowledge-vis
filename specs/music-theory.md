## Causal contract
1. Semitone distance maps to interval names and twelve-tone equal-temperament frequency ratios.
2. Scale degrees map a major or natural-minor mode to semitone positions from the tonic.
3. Roman-numeral chords expose tonic, predominant or dominant function and chord tones.
4. Cadences make tension direction explicit: V–I, IV–I, ii–V and V–vi.

## Scope
A static theory model without generated audio. It does not model tuning systems beyond 12-TET, voice leading or full harmonic analysis.
