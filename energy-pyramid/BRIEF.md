## Steps
**0 Sunlight in.** Knows: plants use light. Question: how much energy starts the chain? Input η; seek producer energy = 5,000,000 × η kJ/m²/yr. Controls: η slider changes the short bar. Holds: example solar input.
**1 Shrinking by level.** Knows: producers. Question: what remains at each level? Input efficiency, level; seek level k = producers × eff^(k−1), fates (70% respiration / 30% decomposers of loss). Controls: efficiency reshapes the four bars; level slider highlights one bar and its split. Holds: one common efficiency, assumed split.
**2 Room at the top.** Knows: level energies. Question: why are top predators few? Input efficiency, level; seek area = 365,000 / E. Controls: slider levels 1–6, hectare squares grow. Holds: fox-sized need 365,000 kJ/yr (illustrative).
**3 Three pyramids.** Knows: energy pyramid. Question: can a pyramid invert? Input ecosystem; seek which of numbers/biomass/production is inverted. Controls: grassland, forest, ocean. Holds: illustrative data, log-scale widths; production = biomass × turnover.
**4 Diet and people fed.** Knows: efficiency loss. Question: why does a plant-based diet feed more? Input meat share, conversion efficiency; seek land per person = need × [(1−m) + m/conv] / yield. Controls: dot grid for people per 10 ha. Holds: need 3.8e6 kJ/yr, yield 6e7 kJ/ha/yr.
**5 Recap.** Quiz: 10% three times = 0.1%. Links: photosynthesis#step2, population-ecology#step3.
