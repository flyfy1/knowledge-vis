## The one question

为什么随机的东西离开起点的距离按时间的平方根增长？ Every chapter answers part of it.

## Chapters, models, limits

1. 醉汉走路 (ch1). Lab: 200 coin-flip walkers with ±√n and ±2√n envelopes; measured share inside ±√n against the exact
   binomial value (72.9% at n = 100). Diagram: four-step Pascal lattice (counts from C(n,k)). Limit stated: RMS √n vs
   mean |S_n| ≈ 0.80√n.
2. 方差相加 (ch2). Lab: 4,000 sums of three step laws with variance 1 (coin, uniform, lopsided ±); width √n for all,
   skewness 1.15/√n for the lopsided one. Links binomial-normal, concept:gaussian, concept:variance (CLT not re-taught).
3. 布朗运动 (ch3). Lab: 300 particles, D from Stokes–Einstein with water viscosity by the Vogel fit; measured per-axis
   ⟨x²⟩ vs 2Dt; times to spread 10 µm / 1 mm / 1 cm. Diagram: Einstein → Perrin argument chain. Limit: small-molecule D
   only an order of magnitude; ballistic regime at short times.
4. 几何布朗运动 (ch4). Lab: 30 GBM paths (log axis), median band, P(loss), T* = σ²/(μ−σ²/2)² (16 years at 7%/20%).
   Diagram: +10%/−10% → 99 on log scale. Annualisation 1.28% × √252 = 20.3% (NASDAQ). Not registered as a concept
   (owned by a-fair-price-for-a-bet#ch5 per TASK-econ-series §4.J); links to that draft by raw href (#ch4, #ch5, #ch6, #ch8).
5. 市场真的是随机游走吗 (ch5). Lab A: NASDAQ Composite log returns at 1/5/21/63 days vs normal (kurtosis 12.3 → 9.8 →
   7.0 → 5.1; 96 days beyond 4σ vs 0.9 expected). Lab B: overlapping variance ratios by era with a 400-shuffle 95% band
   (1971–89 VR21 2.37 above; 1990–2009 inside; 2010– 0.78 below). Hill tail index 3.4 (top 1%) / 3.2 (top 2.5%).
   Diagram: VR>1 / =1 / <1. Efficient-markets claim stated in one paragraph only, deferred to the stock-valuation essay
   (plain text, not yet live). Limit: shuffle band ignores volatility clustering, so it is too narrow.
6. 赌徒破产 (ch6). Lab: timid (unit) vs bold play, both solved exactly by Gaussian elimination; closed form checked
   against the solve at every N step 10 and five p values. 50→100 at 18/37: 6.3% vs 48.6%; fair game 2,500 bets.
   Diagram: absorbing barriers. Links markov-chains#step5, risk-pooling, expected-value.
7. 期权和扩散模型 (ch7). Lab: DDPM forward process (linear β 1e-4→0.02, T = 1000) on a two-peak toy; peaks merge at
   step 395. Diagram: forward/reverse. No computing/ml diffusion page exists, so none is linked.
8. 前沿在哪 (ch8). Prose only: 已确立 / 已演示 / 某方声称, plus three usable questions.
